Theme Ladder

About this prototype

  • The data is fictional: 60 trading days and 22 themes, generated with a distribution where "most days are flat", at close to real scale.
  • Level rules follow the PRD decisions: % change ≥ +3% is up, ≤ −3% is down, in between is flat (8-5); the algorithm stops at level 1 (8-4); dropping off the list means out, and returning requires being manually marked level 1 again (8-3); level 0 is not shown (8-2).
  • The Y axis draws only levels that have data (8-9); same-level themes stack vertically, sorted by that day's gain, descending (8-10); the cell area scrolls vertically inside the panel (7.2-24).
  • Swipe left/right to see earlier days, with no requests sent (8-7); drag "Panel width" and the column count adapts between 5 and 16 (8-8).
  • Hover to see the popover; click = broadcast the selected theme + light up its full trail, auto-scrolling to the trail; click again to cancel (8-11).
  • The "Include ST" toggle affects only the counts in the popover; level and % change stay the same (8-12).
  • Bull/bear cycle band: consecutive equal values merge into segments; no future columns are drawn (8-13).
  • Of the empty states in §3.1.4, "a theme not listed on some day" and "a band segment not entered" show up naturally in the data (days off the list have no cell, and the early band is gray); this prototype has no toggles for "too few days with a theme list" or "no leader tagged".
